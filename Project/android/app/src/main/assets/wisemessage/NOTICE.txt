WiseMessage is a separate Chinese SMS spam/scam research project.

The native BERT encoder and checkpoint mapping were adapted from the local
LinkWise MindSpore implementation. Apache License 2.0 terms are in LICENSE.txt.
WiseMessage does not import or require the LinkWise project at runtime.

The pretrained MiniRBT model is by the Joint Laboratory of HIT and iFLYTEK
Research (HFL), released under Apache License 2.0. Model source, immutable
revision, hashes and discarded pretraining components are recorded in
artifacts/pretrained/manifest.json.

Dataset terms differ from the source-code and pretrained-model license.
See data/prepared/source_documentation.json and data/prepared/manifest.json.
The revised message-domain dataset is documented separately in
data/prepared_domain_v2/source_documentation.json and its manifest. It adds
partially labelled Mudou unwanted SMS and FGRC-SCD generated message-form
examples. FGRC-SCD's dataset card declares MIT; it describes generated data,
not independently captured real SMS. Existing source-specific restrictions
continue to apply to the combined model.
The current dataset combination and trained artifacts are a research prototype;
unresolved dataset permissions and domain-validation gaps remain.
